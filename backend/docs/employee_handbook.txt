Brightwave Company Employee Handbook (Sample)

Working hours
The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. There is a one-hour unpaid lunch break, taken between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Employees may choose a flexible start time between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM if their manager agrees.

Leave
Every full-time employee gets 15 days of paid vacation leave and 7 days of sick leave each year. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. A vacation longer than 3 days must be requested at least 14 days ahead.

Remote work
After finishing the 3-month probation period, employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. While working from home, they must be reachable on the company chat during core hours, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Dress code
The office dress code is business casual. Fridays are casual days, and jeans are allowed. Meetings with clients require formal clothes.

Performance reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in June and in December. A review looks at goals, teamwork, and feedback from coworkers.

Equipment
A laptop is given to each employee on the first day. If equipment is lost or damaged, it must be reported to the IT desk within 24 hours.

Expense claims
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days. Meals during business travel are paid back up to 1,500 pesos per day.
