Northpeak Return and Refund Policy

Return window
Items can be returned within 45 days of the delivery date. They must be unused and in the original packaging.

Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days after we receive the item.

Electronics
Electronics have a shorter return window of 21 days. Opened laptops are charged a 20 percent restocking fee.

Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, software licenses, custom orders, and food items cannot be returned.

Warranty claims
Electronics come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty. To make a warranty claim, first ask support for a Return Merchandise Authorization number, called an RMA number. An example is RMA-9082. Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package before sending it.

Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 72 hours and attach photos. We will send a replacement at no cost.

Return shipping
Return shipping is free for defective items, and for change-of-mind returns on orders above 5,000 pesos. Other change-of-mind returns pay a flat shipping fee of 250 pesos.

Contact
Email support@northpeak.example. The hotline is open from 9 AM to 9 PM.
