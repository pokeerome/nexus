Solmart Employee Handbook (Sample)

Working hours
The standard working hours are 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Tuesday to Saturday. There is a 90-minute unpaid break, taken between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM. Shift swaps need approval from the store supervisor.

Leave
Every full-time employee gets 12 days of paid vacation leave and 5 days of sick leave each year. Up to 3 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. A vacation longer than 2 days must be requested at least 30 days ahead.

Remote work
Store staff cannot work from home. Office staff may work from home 1 day per week after 12 months with the company. While working from home, they must be reachable on the company chat during core hours, from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

Dress code
Store staff wear the company uniform. Office staff dress business casual.

Performance reviews
Reviews happen once a year, in January. A review looks at sales results, teamwork, and feedback from customers.

Equipment
Store staff receive a handheld scanner and a name badge. If equipment is lost or damaged, it must be reported to the store supervisor within 12 hours.

Expense claims
Receipts must be submitted within 45 days. Meals during business travel are paid back up to 1,000 pesos per day.
