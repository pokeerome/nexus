Home Solar Panel Installation Guide

Step 1: Check the roof
Before anything else, inspect the roof. It must be able to carry an extra 20 kilograms per square meter. Repair any damaged roof sections first.

Step 2: Choose the position
In the northern hemisphere, panels should face south. The best tilt angle is close to the latitude of your location.

Step 3: Fix the mounting rails
Attach the rails to the roof rafters with lag bolts. Cover every bolt hole with roofing sealant to stop water leaks.

Step 4: Place the panels
Clip the panels onto the rails and connect them to each other in a series string.

Step 5: Install the inverter
Place the inverter near the main electrical panel. The inverter changes the direct current (DC) from the panels into alternating current (AC), which is what the house uses.

Step 6: Connect to the grid
A permit and an inspection are needed before the system is connected to the power grid.

Safety rules
- Switch off the system before any work.
- Wear a safety harness when working higher than 2 meters.
- Never work on the roof when it is wet or very windy.
- Cover the panels with a blanket during work, because panels produce voltage whenever there is daylight.

Maintenance
Clean the panels twice a year with a soft brush and water. Check the inverter display once a month. A panel lasts about 25 years, and its output drops by about 0.5 percent each year.
