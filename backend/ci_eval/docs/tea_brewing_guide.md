# Home Tea Brewing Guide

## Green tea
Use water at 80 degrees Celsius, not fully boiling. Use about 2 grams of leaves for every 100 milliliters of water. Steep for 2 minutes. Do not steep longer, because green tea turns bitter quickly.

## Black tea
Use water at 95 to 100 degrees Celsius. Use about 3 grams of leaves for every 200 milliliters of water. Steep for 4 minutes.

## Oolong tea
Use water at 90 degrees Celsius. Use about 5 grams of leaves for every 150 milliliters of water. Steep for 3 minutes. Good oolong leaves can be steeped again 4 to 5 times.

## White tea
Use water at 85 degrees Celsius and steep for 5 minutes.

## Herbal tea
Use fully boiling water and steep for 7 minutes.

## Cold brew tea
Use 5 grams of leaves for every 500 milliliters of cold water. Steep in the fridge for 8 to 10 hours, then strain.

## Storing tea
Keep tea in an airtight container, away from light, moisture, and strong smells. Use it within 12 months.

## Fixing problems
- Bitter tea usually means the water was too hot or the tea steeped for too long.
- Weak tea means you need more leaves or a longer steep.
