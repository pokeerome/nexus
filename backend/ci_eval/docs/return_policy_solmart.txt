Solmart Return Policy

Return window
Items can be returned within 14 days of purchase. They must be unused, with the receipt.

Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 business days. Cash purchases are refunded in cash at the store.

Electronics
Electronics have a return window of 7 days, and opened electronics cannot be returned.

Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, underwear, personal care items, and clearance items cannot be returned.

Warranty claims
Electronics come with a 6-month warranty. For a warranty claim, ask the service desk for a return slip number. An example is SM-RET-310. Keep the return slip together with your receipt.

Damaged items
If an item is damaged, tell the service desk on the same day.

Where to return
Solmart does not accept returns by mail. Items must be returned in person at any Solmart store.

Contact
Call the hotline from 8 AM to 6 PM.
