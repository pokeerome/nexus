Family Earthquake Safety Plan

Before an earthquake
- Fasten tall furniture, shelves, and water heaters to the wall.
- Keep heavy objects on low shelves.
- Learn where the main water, power, and gas shutoffs are.
- Practice earthquake drills with your family twice a year.
- Keep sturdy shoes and a flashlight next to every bed.

Emergency kit
Prepare a bag for every family. It should have:
- Drinking water: 4 liters per person per day, enough for 5 days.
- Canned food and a manual can opener.
- Whistle, dust masks, and a battery-powered radio.
- First aid kit and any regular medicines.
- Copies of IDs and important papers in a sealed plastic folder.

During an earthquake
- Drop, Cover, and Hold On: get down on your hands and knees, cover your head and neck under a sturdy table, and hold on until the shaking stops.
- Stay away from windows, mirrors, and anything that can fall.
- Do not use elevators.
- If you are outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, trees, and power lines.
- If you are in a car, pull over and stay inside.

After an earthquake
- Expect aftershocks, and be ready to drop, cover, and hold on again.
- Check yourself and others for injuries.
- If you smell gas, open the windows and leave the building right away. Do not use matches or light switches.
- Send text messages instead of calling, to keep phone lines free.
- If you live near the coast and the shaking was strong or long, move to higher ground right away, because a tsunami may follow.
