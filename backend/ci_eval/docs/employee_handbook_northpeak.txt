Northpeak Employee Handbook (Sample)

Working hours
The standard working hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday. There is a one-hour unpaid lunch break, taken between 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM. Employees may choose a flexible start time between 7:30 AM and 9:30 AM if their manager agrees.

Leave
Every full-time employee gets 20 days of paid vacation leave and 10 days of sick leave each year. Up to 10 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year. A vacation longer than 5 days must be requested at least 21 days ahead.

Remote work
After finishing the 6-month probation period, employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. While working from home, they must be reachable on the company chat during core hours, from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM.

Dress code
The office dress code is smart casual. Thursdays are casual days, and sneakers are allowed. Meetings with clients require a suit or other formal clothes.

Performance reviews
Reviews happen three times a year, in February, June, and October. A review looks at goals, teamwork, and feedback from coworkers.

Equipment
A laptop and a monitor are given to each employee on the first day. If equipment is lost or damaged, it must be reported to the IT desk within 48 hours.

Expense claims
Receipts must be submitted within 14 days. Meals during business travel are paid back up to 2,000 pesos per day.
