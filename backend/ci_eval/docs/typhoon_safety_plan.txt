Family Typhoon Safety Plan

Before the typhoon
- Follow official weather bulletins from the national weather agency and your local government.
- Fix loose roof sheets and trim tree branches near the house.
- Charge phones and power banks.
- Move valuables and appliances to higher floors.
- Find out where your nearest evacuation center is and how to get there.

Emergency go-bag
Prepare a bag for every family. It should have:
- Drinking water: 3 liters per person per day, enough for 3 days.
- Canned food and a manual can opener.
- Flashlight, spare batteries, and a battery-powered radio.
- First aid kit and any regular medicines.
- Copies of IDs and important papers, sealed inside a waterproof bag.
- Some cash, because ATMs may not work.

During the typhoon
- Stay indoors and keep away from windows.
- Do not walk or drive through floodwater. Just 15 centimeters of moving water can knock a person down.
- If water enters the house, switch off the main power and unplug appliances.
- Evacuate right away when local officials give the order.

After the typhoon
- Stay away from fallen power lines and report them.
- If you are not sure the water is clean, boil it for at least 1 minute before drinking.
- Take photos of any damage for insurance and for aid requests.
- Watch out for broken glass and weak walls before going back inside.
