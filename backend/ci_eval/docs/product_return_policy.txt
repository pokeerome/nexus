Brightwave Store Return and Refund Policy

Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. They must be unused and in the original packaging.

Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days after we receive the item.

Electronics
Electronics have a shorter return window of 14 days. Opened laptops are charged a 15 percent restocking fee.

Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, digital downloads, personalized items, and perishable goods cannot be returned.

Warranty claims
Electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. To make a warranty claim, first ask support for a Return Merchandise Authorization number, called an RMA number. An example is RMA-4471. Write the RMA number clearly on the outside of the package before sending it.

Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours and attach photos. We will send a free replacement.

Return shipping
Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns, the customer pays a flat shipping fee of 150 pesos.

Contact
Email returns@brightwave.example. The hotline is open from 8 AM to 8 PM.
