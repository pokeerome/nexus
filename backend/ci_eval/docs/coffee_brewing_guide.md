# Home Coffee Brewing Guide

## Pour-over
Use a ratio of 1 part coffee to 16 parts water. For example, use 20 grams of coffee with 320 grams of water. The water should be 92 to 96 degrees Celsius. Grind the beans to a medium size, similar to table salt. Start with a 30-second bloom: pour about twice the weight of the coffee in water, then wait. The full brew should take 2.5 to 3 minutes.

## French press
Use a coarse grind and a ratio of 1 to 15. Steep for 4 minutes, then press the plunger down slowly.

## Espresso
Use a fine grind. A common recipe is 18 grams of coffee in and 36 grams of espresso out, in 25 to 30 seconds, at 9 bars of pressure.

## Cold brew
Use a coarse grind and make a concentrate with a ratio of 1 to 8. Steep it in the fridge for 12 to 16 hours, then dilute with water or milk.

## Storing beans
Keep beans in an airtight container, away from light and heat. Do not put beans in the fridge. Use them within 4 weeks of the roast date for the best taste.

## Fixing problems
- Sour coffee means it is under-extracted. Grind finer or use hotter water.
- Bitter coffee means it is over-extracted. Grind coarser or shorten the brew time.
